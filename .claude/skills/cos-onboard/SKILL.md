# Onboard

Goal: in one short session, turn the templates in `state/` into a working picture of
Sixten's world. Do the homework first so the interview is short.

## 1. Homework (before asking anything)
- Calendar: past 14 days and next 14 days. Note recurring meetings, who appears
  most, how much time is in meetings, and when focus time exists.
- Gmail: last 14 days of threads Sixten **sent** or replied to. Who do they write
  to most? What threads are still open (Sixten asked a question or promised
  something)?
- Drive / Notion: the 10 most recently edited docs — these hint at active projects.

## 2. Interview (one message, numbered, max 6 questions)
Show what you inferred and ask Sixten to correct it rather than starting blank:
1. Timezone and working hours (propose what the calendar suggests).
2. The 3 things that matter most this quarter — propose candidates from the
   homework.
3. Top 3 for this week.
4. VIPs whose mail must always surface.
5. What may be auto-archived; anything off limits.
6. Writing voice for drafts (offer a sample based on Sixten's sent mail).

## 3. Write state
- Fill `state/priorities.md` and `state/preferences.md`; remove the TEMPLATE lines.
- Add the 5–10 most frequent contacts to `state/people.md`.
- Seed `state/commitments.md` with open promises found in sent mail (owner `me`)
  and things others promised Sixten. Cite the thread subject as source.
- Ask before creating any Gmail labels (`cos/read-later`, `cos/receipts`,
  `cos/waiting`).

## 4. Close
Summarize in ≤8 bullets what you now know, list what's still unknown, and suggest
the daily schedule (morning brief time, end-of-day time, weekly review day). Offer
to set these up as scheduled Routines — don't create them without a yes.
